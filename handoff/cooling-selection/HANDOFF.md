# Cooling selection handoff

- Engine: `crates/novo-thermal/src/cooling.rs`. Need = loss - k*A*(target - ambient) (IEC 60890 surface, `novo_layout::sizing`). Fan flow = FAN_FACTOR*need/delta, derated 0.9 (fan) or 0.7 (filter fan); units need 1.1x. Fans are out when delta < 3 K or the enclosure must stay closed.
- Products: parts with `airflow_m3h` / `cooling_capacity_w` (optional `cooling_kind`), the person's `cooling.csv` (`name;kind;airflow_m3h;cooling_w`, under %APPDATA%\KoperCAD), then generic planning sizes labelled "planning size".
- App: `crates/novo-app/src/app/heat_cooling.rs`; shown in `view3d_heat.rs` (3D Heat panel) and the legend of `heat.rs` (Cabinet Heat view). Result carries the enclosure (`Solution.enclosure`). Report lines go through `HeatFact.cooling` into the standards report.
- Wire heat (#1323) is included: `Solution.duct_loss_w` (sum of duct obstacle losses) is added to the device loss in `basis_for` and shown as its own term. Heat-up over time: the selection reads the shown frame, so a frame below full load can propose less; today runs start at full load.
- QA: `qa/scenarios/44-cooling-selection-1366.qa` and `-1920.qa` (fixture `qa/fixtures/cabinet-wire-heat.novo`, Quick run). They cover the finished run, the proposal (target held at 35 C), Must stay closed, scrolling to the caveat and the Cabinet Temperature panel. The fixture only loses 14 W, so the hot case holds the target at the outside temperature; real proposals are covered by the hand-calculated tests.
- Known limit: the Cabinet-panel picture comes from a Cabinet-view re-run that was at 11 percent (not converged) when shot, because software rendering cannot finish a Cabinet run in the harness. It shows the section before the run finished; it is not evidence of a finished-run Cabinet panel. Re-shoot it when a run finishes.
