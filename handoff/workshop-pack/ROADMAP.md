# Workshop pack: roadmap

Scope: one button that writes the hand-workshop bundle (proposal 12 of audit/creative/proposals-round1.md).

Done (PR from `claude/project-thread-o9kk2b`):
- 1:1 drilling template per mounting plate (and door or wall with holes): cross hairs, hole sizes, cut-outs, device outlines, tiled on A4 or A3 with 10 mm overlap, grey registration crosses, coordinate ticks, a 100 mm scale-check ruler on every sheet.
- Overview sheet per surface: map of the tiles, print and lay-out steps, hole list by size, cut-outs, devices without hole data, unreadable hole patterns.
- `export.workshop` and the Bench output window button write `<project> workshop/`: template, cut list, ferrules, labels, assembly checklist.

Next, in order:
1. Clip the template to the plate edge so the area outside the plate is shaded (cut line vs waste).
2. Mirror option for templates used from the back of a plate or on a door seen from outside.
3. Letter size (US) paper once the NFPA pack exists.
4. A printed-ruler calibration: let the user type the measured ruler length and store a print scale correction.
5. Per-device hole labels with the tag (not only numbers).
