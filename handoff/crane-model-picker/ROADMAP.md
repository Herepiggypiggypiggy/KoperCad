# Crane model picker and motion: roadmap

Scope: "Use my crane model" chain (lane R3 of crane sim realism). The person points Hoist in 3D at a STEP file of their own crane; the picture shows it; part roles drive its motion.

1. Part 1 (merged, #1965): `kopercad-cli parts step-parts` splits a STEP file into solids and guesses a role from each name (`novo-interop` `part3d::build::placed_parts`, `part3d::crane_roles`).
2. Part 2 (this thread): the picker. Simulate > Hoist in 3D > "Crane model..." reads the file on a worker thread and shows it in place of the generic crane; "Parts" lists the solids with their role, the file's up axis and a turn; roles are editable; the project keeps the path, orientation and edited roles (`crane3d.model*` info properties, one undo step "Crane picture").
3. Part 3 (built, PR follows part 2): roles drive motion (`crane_motion.rs`). Every solid joins the Frame, the Bridge, the Trolley or the Hook group by its role and by what it touches; bridge and trolley travel come from the model's own rails and girder; wheels turn with the distance rolled, the drum with the rope paid out (2 falls); the hook solids rise, swing about the drum and stop under it and above the floor; two ropes, the load, the slack heap, the limit flags and the lamps are drawn like the generic crane. A model with only a hoist unit (no rails, no girders) puts its hook and hoist solids into the generic crane (`crane_user.rs`, from the crane-realism thread's #1971 idea).

Later: a per-solid override of the group (today a solid with no motion role takes the group of the nearest one), hover highlight in the hoist-unit mode, a drum that turns in the hoist-unit mode, colours and names from the file's own style data, a ZIP of STEP files, part motion limits from the model's rails, S4 crane kinds.
