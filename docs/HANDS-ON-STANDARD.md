# Hands-on check: drawing standard

1. Open Ctrl+K, type `standard`, press Enter: the Project settings window
   opens with IEC 60617 selected.
2. Choose NFPA 79: the window title gains " · NFPA". Placed symbols do not
   change yet; the window offers "Switch N placed symbols to NFPA".
3. Press Q (hold) for the wheel: Breaker no longer offers RCBO or Surge in
   NFPA, and a picked item attaches the NFPA drawing.
4. Press the Switch button: the placed symbols change to the NFPA drawings in
   one step. Ctrl+Z puts the drawings back (the standard stays NFPA).
5. Devices with no NFPA drawing stay as drawn and are listed in the window.
6. Choose IEC again and switch back the same way.
