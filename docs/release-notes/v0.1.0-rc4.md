Release candidate 4 of the KoperCAD beta. Windows 10/11 only. No admin rights are needed.

## Files

- `KoperCAD-*-setup.exe`: per-user installer (installs under your profile).
- `KoperCAD-*-portable.zip`: unzip anywhere and double-click `run.cmd`; includes `kopercad-cli.exe`. A file `kopercad.portable` next to the exe keeps settings, logs and recovery files in the `data` folder beside it.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums of the two files above. `BUILD_INFO.txt`: version, commit and build time.

## Try these first

1. **Installer and first start (needs a real Windows PC).** Install without any admin prompt. Start from the Start menu and by double-clicking a project file. Check Help > About (maker, licence, "Copy version info", "Open log folder", the graphics adapter line). If the window stays black or the program does not start, use the Start menu entry **KoperCAD (safe graphics)** (portable zip: `run-safe-graphics.cmd`). Uninstall once and read the question about removing settings, logs and recovery files: the default is No, and your projects are never deleted. Install a second build over a running copy.
2. **Build mode.** Step through building a cabinet at the bench: one step per device with its position on the plate, door, wall, roof or floor (door measured from the hinge edge), ticks with name and time, claiming a step per person, a problem editor with photos, and "Print sheet..." for a build sheet PDF. Desk and tablet should run the same build.
3. **Machine options.** Mark parts of a project as options: left-out objects appear ghosted on the schematic, View > Layers has a switch, and the Reports lists have an Option column. Cabinet, heat and Commissioning follow the chosen options.
4. **Review notes.** Comments on pages and objects with open/resolved status, replies, search, an "N1" bubble on the canvas and in the exported PDF, and a review-notes list. The handover package only includes the review notes if you tick them (off by default, so internal remarks do not reach the customer).
5. **3D heat: vents and fans.** The Vents and fans panel draws openings in the walls, lets the fans reach the solver, colours the airflow by speed, saves the openings in the project, and lets you drag them in 3D. Cooling advice offers a fan together with its grille; the **Cooler layout** button in the Heat panel opens the Cabinet view and suggests a placement that takes the heat into account.
6. **EMC design aid.** Check > EMC check, plus EMC views in the 3D cabinet and on the cabinet plate: keep-clear bands, a low-frequency magnetic field estimate and a field slice. It is switched on per project.
7. **Ask Claude as co-engineer (optional).** A draft workspace with plan and question cards, 17 actions, all undoable. Needs your own key: see the caveats.
8. **Large projects.** Open a project with several hundred pages: Commissioning, the bench packet, Reports counts and the page order should stay responsive. Reports counts show "-" until the count for the current state is known.
9. **Learn.** The Learn window is now a suite with a side rail, a home with a recommended lesson, topics, search and level chips, and 11 new lessons including a Machines track. Ctrl+K "continue lesson" resumes where you stopped.
10. **Cabinet layout.** Place all now takes heat into account (the preview may move hot parts and says how many were placed differently). Rails run through the middle of their devices and there is a "Centre rails on devices" button. Rows and ducts follow the 35 mm rail band.
11. **Workshop pack, wire numbering, parts import, Single-line diagram, Standards report, Cable runs in 3D.** As in rc3, if you did not try them yet.

## Changes you will notice

- **The project file extension is now `.kopercad`.** Files named `.novo` from older builds still open, and saving writes `.kopercad`. Earlier test builds cannot list `.kopercad` files in their Open dialog.
- **Delete the Starter folder** in My macros (or Company macros) and reopen the macro browser, to get the fully wired Starter macros. An installed copy is never replaced automatically.
- **Duct placement changed.** Ducts now go over the rail and rows have a minimum gap of 57.5 mm. In a nearly full plate a separation may be dropped or a device left unplaced; both are reported. Plans saved earlier keep the old rail position until you run Place all again, and sheets printed before this change should be printed again.
- **Power-wire heat in ducts.** A planning upper value: copper at the insulation maximum temperature, no diversity unless you state it, neutral and PE not counted, and part-load scaling is linear, so it overestimates.
- **Wire production data.** The wire list and bench output carry bundle, sequence, track, direction, end processing, D-names and sleeve A/B.
- **Live view** says when no supply is defined. **Ctrl+K** finds "cut-out", "drill template" and "door" (Export workshop pack), "duct heat" and "wire heat", slotted ducts, and the PLC input card.
- **Report tables** size their columns to the text. The PLC I/O list import keeps page names.
- **A recovered copy of a damaged project** keeps the damaged file's extension.
- Files written by earlier builds are tested to still open and edit.

## Caveats

- **Not code-signed.** Windows SmartScreen may warn on first start: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **Not verified on real Windows hardware.** The automated checks run on Linux and on a Windows build server without a GPU. The installer is checked there (per-user install, no admin rights, the app starts), but the new installer questions, update over a running copy, portable `data` folder, the safe-graphics renderer, double-click opening, HiDPI at 150% and 200% and the absence of a console flash can only be proven on a real PC. Most of the new screens (Build mode, Review notes, Vents, EMC, Learn suite) have only been seen in Linux screenshots. Two new interactions were tested with replay and unit tests only, not with a real keyboard and mouse: the Place-wheel route in a lesson and Page Down with the pointer over the lesson card.
- **Ask Claude needs your own API key and has only been tested with a scripted model**, not the real API. It is off until you turn it on, it sends the data scope you allow plus what you press, and every edit it suggests can be undone. The steps for a real-key test are in `docs/CLAUDE-ASSISTANT-TEST.md`.
- **EMC is a rule-based design aid, not a test result and not a compliance statement.** 9 of the 18 design rules are built; the rest are listed under "Not checked". Distances and field levels are order-of-magnitude estimates with stated assumptions, the IEC 61000-4-8 levels are unverified, and the plate bands are drawn at most a fifth of the plate's short side (the finding card shows the real planning distance).
- **Heat, airflow and cooling results are models, not measurements.** The 2D heat model, the 3D CFD (a reduced model for large cabinets) and the cooler layout use planning values and are not validated against a real enclosure. A fan proposal card has no picture yet. Openings in the 3D panel are labelled "(model)".
- **Wire sizes, colours, breaking capacity checks and standard clause numbers are planning values.** The IEC 60204-1 clause numbers are the authors' reading, not checked against a licensed copy. The short-circuit figures use the supply data you state (the example's 400 V / 6 kA is illustrative only) and leave out motor contribution and busbar impedance.
- **Build mode:** Large mode below 1200 pt has no picture; a tick you undid can return from the desk copy when copies merge; grouping power, control and earth is usual practice, not read from the schematic. The bubble for a review note sits on the page frame corner and the delete bin has no label.
- **Safety symbols carry no safety category or PL/SIL evaluation.**
- Two wire-colour pixel checks and the `novo-cfd` simulation tests do not run on the Windows build server (no GPU; Linux covers the simulation tests). Time budgets in the large-project tests are wall-clock.
